FAQs

What happens when I update my email address (or mobile number)?

Your login email id (or mobile number) changes, too. You'll receive all your account related communication on your updated email address (or mobile number).

When will my Flipkart account be updated with the new email address (or mobile number)?

It happens instantly. You can start using your new email address (or mobile number) to login after you confirm it via the OTP sent to your new email address (or mobile number).
